Design Proposal for Instagram Engagement Feature

Design Philosophy

The "Ask Counselor Sama" section replaces the generic "Contact Us" form with an Instagram-focused engagement strategy. It transforms your website from a passive contact point into an active content creation funnel, where visitors submit wellness questions that Sama answers in her Instagram reels.

🎯 Strategic Purpose

Shift from generic inquiries to curated content. Every submission feeds your Instagram growth engine.

✨ Aesthetic Direction

Warm, inviting, conversational. Less corporate form, more intimate fireside chat. Uses your brand palette: navy + burgundy with rose accents.

📱 Instagram Loop

Form → Email submission → Sama creates reel → Link back to Instagram. Creates a viral feedback loop driving followers.

🎨 Form Field Strategy

Minimal required fields (just email + question). Optional name. Topics dropdown for content categorization. Consent checkbox for Instagram usage.

Interactive Details & Animation

Form Focus States

When users click an input, the border transitions to burgundy with a subtle shadow. The background shifts from off-white to pure white, creating a clear visual focus state. This improves usability and draws attention naturally.

Button Hover Effect

The submit button lifts up 2px on hover with an enhanced shadow. The gradient shifts slightly for a 3D effect. On click, it returns to baseline immediately. Creates a satisfying, tactile interaction.

Success State Animation

After submission, the form fades out. The success container fades in with a subtle scale animation (1.02 → 1.0). The checkmark icon bounces gently for delightful feedback.

Mobile Responsiveness

On small screens, the 2-column desktop layout stacks vertically. Left column messaging becomes a header section. Form takes full width. All touch targets remain 44px+ for accessibility.

Instagram CTA Integration

After successful submission, a prominent "Follow on Instagram" button appears with the Instagram gradient background. This closes the loop: question → answer → larger audience → more Instagram followers.

Accessibility Features

All form inputs have clear labels. Color contrast meets WCAG AA standards. Focus states are visible. The required field indicator (*) is semantic. Error messages will be announced to screen readers.

Why This Design Works

1. Two-Column Desktop Layout: Separates psychology (why they should submit) from mechanics (the form). The left side builds emotional connection, the right side is frictionless form entry. On mobile, it becomes a single column with the messaging as a hero section.

2. Minimal Form Fields: Only email + topic + question required. Optional name. This reduces friction vs. your current 5-field form. Users share more when the barrier is lower, and you still get the info you need.

3. Instagram Badge & Language: "Featured in my reels 🎬" immediately communicates the value prop. Users aren't filling out a generic contact form—they're submitting content ideas. This psychological shift increases quality submissions.

4. Warm, Conversational Tone: "How should I address you?" vs. "First Name". "What's on your mind?" vs. "Message". Uses Gilda Display serif (luxury/therapy feel) paired with Nunito Sans body (modern, readable).

5. Consent Checkbox: "I give permission for my question to be featured... anonymously or with my name." This builds trust and clarifies the Instagram use case upfront. Legal + practical.

6. Success State Redirect:** After submission, users see the Instagram follow CTA. This completes the loop: form → gratitude → Instagram follow. Drives direct engagement metrics.

7. Brand Coherence: Navy + burgundy + rose accents match your existing site. The gradient backgrounds use your color story. Typography hierarchy (Gilda Display for headers) reinforces your premium wellness positioning.
