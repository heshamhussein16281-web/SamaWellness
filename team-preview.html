Key Benefits of This Restructuring

Matchmaking Speed

Visitors instantly see therapeutic approach + specializations without clicking. They can decide in 2 seconds if this therapist is a match.

Professional Context

The approach statement captures their style/philosophy—this is what people actually want to know about a therapist's work.

Visual Hierarchy

Clear sections: photo → identity → approach → specialties → optional depth. No cramming. No truncation of important info.

All Breakpoints

Stacked information sections work perfectly on mobile, tablet, and desktop. No layout shifts.

Elegant Design

Subtle dividers, larger specialization tags, and italicized approach text create visual interest without clutter.

Maintained Aesthetics

Stays true to Sama Wellness's calm, professional aesthetic. No aggressive design changes—just better organization.
